La Storia

A little piece of Italy,
right on Notre-Dame

Café de' Mercanti wasn't born from a business plan. It started with Gianni's obsession for doing espresso the right way — the way his family taught him in Italy. The kind of coffee that doesn't need flavoured syrups to taste good.

Walk in, and it feels like you've stepped into a neighbourhood bar in Rome. The machine hisses, the cups clink, regulars lean on the counter. That's how it should be. We have two locations now — Monkland Village and here in Vieux-Montréal — but the philosophy hasn't changed.
